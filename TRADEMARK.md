Version 1.1, 14 October 2026.

This policy is about names and logos only. It is not part of the code license and does not limit any right that the
GNU AGPL v3.0 gives you: you may copy, change, share and sell the code under [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). The names are
governed by trademark law, which is separate from the code license (term (f) in `LICENSE`).

## The marks

oak993 uses the following as trademarks for nth (not registered at the date of this policy):

- the name **nth** for software, in any capitalisation (nth, Nth, NTH);
- the **nth icon**: the "n" glyph with a bead on a metal tile (`assets-src/brand/nth-icon-b.svg` and the app icon made

Use "TM", not "(R)", if you mark them.

- The names and logos of AI providers and other services that nth works with belong to their owners.

## Official builds

An official nth build is one that oak993 publishes at https://github.com/Oak993/nth (releases and tags
`nth-<version>`) or through nth's own update feed, https://github.com/Oak993/marrow-releases. Every other copy is
unofficial, even if it is unmodified. If you find a copy that claims to be official but was not published there,
please report it (see "Reporting misuse" below).

## Uses that need no permission

- Talking about nth by name: reviews, articles, tutorials, videos, bug reports, comparisons.
- Truthful statements of origin and compatibility: "based on nth", "a fork of nth", "works with nth".
- The attributions that `LICENSE` requires, word for word: "nth by oak993, https://github.com/Oak993/nth" and
  "Droppy Code by Jordy Spruit (Droppy), https://getdroppycode.app". Keeping them is not a use of the marks as your
  branding.
- Sharing an unmodified official build, or the unmodified source of a release, under the name nth, as long as you keep
  every file and notice as it is, do not bundle other software with it, do not suggest that you are its publisher or
  that oak993 supports your copy, and provide the source as the AGPL requires (a link to the matching `nth-<version>`
  tag is enough while the source stays available there).
- Using the name nth for a build that you make for yourself and do not share.

## Uses that need written permission from oak993

- Naming a product, fork, modified version, service, company, domain name, app-store listing, package (for example on
  winget, NuGet, npm or the Microsoft Store), extension or social-media account with "nth" or one of the feature names
  above, or with a confusingly similar name (for example "nthCode", "nth Pro", "nth 2" or "NTH Studio").
- Using the nth icon, or a changed or similar icon, for anything other than pointing to nth itself.
- Using the marks in a way that suggests that oak993 made, endorses, sponsors or supports your product, service or
  version.
- Using the marks to advertise anything other than an official nth build.
- Using "oak993" as the publisher, signer or author name of anything that oak993 did not publish.

## If you publish a modified version or a fork

The license lets you do this. To stay within trademark law and the origin terms in `LICENSE` (terms (b) and (e)):

1. Give it a clearly different name, your own icon and your own publisher name. Change every user-visible name: window
   titles, the About screen, the installer, Start-menu and desktop shortcuts, the uninstall entry, notifications and the
   executable's version information. Internal code names, such as the `Marrow.*` projects, may stay.
2. Remove the nth icon and nth branding images from your version. Do not remove the attribution text: keep "nth by
   oak993, https://github.com/Oak993/nth" and the Droppy Code attribution where `LICENSE` requires them, and keep
   `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and the third-party notices.
3. Say that it is modified, by whom and when, for example "Modified from nth 1.0.0 by Example, March 2027".
4. Say clearly that your project is not affiliated with or endorsed by oak993, nth, Droppy or Jordy Spruit.
5. Use your own update feed and release pages. If your version keeps nth's update feed, your users will be updated to
   official nth.
6. Follow the license: when you convey your version, provide its Corresponding Source under the GNU AGPL v3.0 as
   sections 4 to 6 of the AGPL require, and, if users interact with your modified version over a network, offer them its
   source as section 13 requires.

## Droppy's marks

nth does not use the Droppy names, logo or icon as its own branding; they appear only in the required attribution.
Forks of nth must also follow Droppy's policy.

## Other companies' names

nth names AI providers and services (for example Claude, Codex or GitHub Copilot) only to say what it works with. Those
names and logos belong to their owners. nth is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of them.

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue at https://github.com/Oak993/nth/issues. If a copy may be harmful (for example a fake installer or a
fake update), report it privately as [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Changes and contact

oak993 may update this policy; the version in this repository applies. Permission that oak993 gives in writing stays
valid as written. To ask for permission, open an issue at https://github.com/Oak993/nth/issues titled "Trademark
permission".
