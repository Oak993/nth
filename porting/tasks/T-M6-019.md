- **Preconditions:** A Droppy-run head (HydraHeadInfo.kind == .droppy) is started with a brief, and later its turn ends.
- **Action:** The brief is sent; the turn finishes.
- **Expected:** When the brief is about to be sent, after the running re-check, executionStartedAt becomes now and executionFinishedAt is cleared, so a head steered on for another turn is timed on the new one. When the head's turn ends, completed, failed or stopped, executionFinishedAt becomes now, before the report is read and before any landing. A head stopped before its brief went out gets neither stamp, and native heads get neither from these paths. Both are stored with the head record under the keys executionStartedAt and executionFinishedAt and decode to nil when absent.
- **Side effects:** Two writes to the head thread's HydraHeadInfo
- **Persistence:** HydraHeadInfo.executionStartedAt and .executionFinishedAt in the head thread's stored record; absent keys decode as nil.
- **Failure cases:** A head whose checkout cannot be resolved returns at startDroppyHead's guard and never gets the start stamp
- **Cancellation:** Stopping the head before the running re-check leaves both stamps untouched.
- **Windows path:** Store both with the same date encoding as the rest of HydraHeadInfo, under the same key names. No platform boundary.
- **Verify by:** interaction test with the offline agent: start a head and assert executionStartedAt set and executionFinishedAt nil when the brief goes out, then executionFinishedAt set when the turn ends; repeat for a failed turn and for a head stopped before it started
