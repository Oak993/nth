- **UI-SIDEBAR-050** Row glide layer (window-wide overlay) / Gliding row ghost (image)
  - geometry: a rectangle interpolated linearly between the departing row's window frame and the destination frame; both face images are anchored at the ghost's top-left at the size each was drawn at; clipped to a 7 pt continuous rounded rectangle and then to the list's own rectangle
  - type: n/a; the faces are flat images rendered at the screen's backing scale factor
  - colour: background Chrome.overlay(interpolated fill, floored at 0.12 * lift) with a black shadow at 0.28 * lift, radius 10, y 4
  - states: departing face fades out between 20 and 65 percent progress; arriving face fades in between 35 and 80 percent; lift rises over the first 12 percent and falls over the last 30 percent; the check crosses between 5 and 95 percent
  - interactions: none; the layer never takes clicks and is hidden from accessibility
- **UI-SIDEBAR-051** Row glide layer / Travelling settle check (icon)
  - geometry: positioned at the ghost's vertical centre, travelling horizontally between 36 pt inside the trailing edge and 16 pt inside the leading edge
  - type: Chrome.inlineIconFont = .system(size: 11, weight: .semibold)
  - colour: Chrome.success
  - states: settling: the filled mark, easing from 1.35 to 1.0 scale over the first half and fading to 0.8 opacity by the end; reopening: the outline mark at constant scale, fading out between 55 and 90 percent
  - interactions: none
