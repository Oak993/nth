## Requirements

### PR-THEME-144 - Hovering a link paragraph raises the pointing hand over links and shows a head's card over a mention, and clears both on exit
- **Source:** DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/LinkHover.swift:1-51 LinkHoverModifier - the shared continuous hover: while active, the hand is pushed when the point is over a link and popped when it leaves, only on change; a head mention under the point whose name, URL and persona all agree shows the head popover anchored to the mention's rect, only when the hovered head changes; anything else hides it; on end the hand is popped and the popover hidden; the same happens on disappear | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/MarkdownView.swift:1119-1120 - `.modifier(LinkHoverModifier(linkView: linkBox, showsHand: $showsHand, hoveredHeadID: $hoveredHeadID, personas: hydraMentionPersonas, targets: targets))` | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:24 - the prose-run view applies the identical `.modifier(LinkHoverModifier(...))` | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/LinkParagraphView.swift:339-350 the text view keeps no tracking areas and no cursor rects of its own, because rebuilding them every scrolled frame was a per-frame tax; the hover comes from the enclosing view instead
- **Preconditions:** A link paragraph or prose run is on screen and the pointer moves over it.
- **Action:** The pointer moves, rests on a mention, and leaves.
- **Expected:** The pointing hand is raised only over real links, and is pushed and popped only when the state changes. Resting over a head mention shows that head's hover card, anchored to the mention's own glyph bounds, after a 250 millisecond delay; moving to a different head replaces it; moving off any mention hides it. Leaving the paragraph, or the paragraph disappearing, pops the cursor and hides the card. The text view itself keeps no tracking areas or cursor rects, so scrolling costs nothing.
- **Side effects:** Pushes and pops the cursor stack; Shows and hides a shared popover
- **Persistence:** not persisted
- **Failure cases:** A mismatch between the hovered name, the target URL and the known persona shows no card.
- **Cancellation:** Moving away before the 250 millisecond delay cancels the card.
- **Windows path:** Cursor changes are driven from the hover handler, not from per-control cursor regions. The hover card itself is specified by the Hydra area; this requirement covers what triggers and clears it.
- **Verify by:** interaction test: the hand appears only over links; the card appears after the delay and clears on scroll-away
