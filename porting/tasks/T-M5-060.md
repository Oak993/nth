### PR-THEME-149 - A prose run renders several blocks as one native text object with explicit paragraph styles, and drops code, tables and rules
- **Source:** DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:90-112 joinedSources() and collect(): paragraph and heading texts, list item texts and their children, and quote children are gathered and joined with newlines; `case .code, .table, .rule: break` | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:114-121 attributed(): every block is appended with `depth: 0, indent: 0, dimmed: dimmed, spacing: 12` | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:124-126 `let base = NSFont.systemFont(ofSize: pointSize)`; `let color = dimmed ? NSColor.labelColor.withAlphaComponent(0.7) : NSColor.labelColor` | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:128-136 a paragraph gets a style with the current indent as both `firstLineHeadIndent` and `headIndent` and `paragraphSpacing = spacing`
- **Preconditions:** A finished message has a run of headings, paragraphs, lists or quotes.
- **Action:** The run is rendered.
- **Expected:** The whole run is one text object whose base point size is the markdown point size rounded to the nearest half point, with no chat zoom. Headings are 1.35, 1.2 and 1.08 times the base point size for levels 1, 2 and 3, rounded, and the base size for levels 4 to 6, with their non-bold non-monospaced runs forced to semibold, 6 points of space before levels 1 and 2 and 2 points before the rest, and 12 points after. A paragraph carries the spacing its caller passes as its space after: 12 points at the top level, 6 points as a child of a list item (a list item's children are appended with spacing 6), and inside a quote whatever the quote itself was given, because a quote passes its caller's spacing through (12 in a top-level quote, 6 in a quote inside a list item). A paragraph is indented by the current indent on its first and following lines. List markers are the ballot-box characters for checkboxes, the number and a full stop when ordered, a bullet at the top level and a hollow bullet deeper, each followed by a tab that lands at 18 points past the item's indent, with the text's continuation lines indented to the same place; the last item of a top-level list carries 12 points after and every other item 6. Quoted content is indented a further 14 points and rendered dimmed, in the label colour with alpha 0.7, with no bar; undimmed text is the label colour. Blocks are separated by a newline.
- **Persistence:** not persisted
- **Failure cases:** None expressed in source.
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** This path draws quotes with an indent and no capsule, and draws bullets as characters rather than as accent-coloured dots, so a finished message looks materially different from the same message mid-stream. That difference is upstream behaviour and must be reproduced, not harmonised.
- **Verify by:** capture comparison: the same message streaming and finished; unit: the heading multipliers and the tab metrics
