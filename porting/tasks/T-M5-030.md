## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-HYDRA-032** Everywhere a head is shown / Head glyph (decoration)
  - geometry: Square at the requested size, default 20. Call sites use 14 (run timings), 16 (stage capsule), 20 (report name row), 22 (heads popover row, multi-head report header), 24 (single-head report header, info popover), 28 (single-head popover header, hover card) and 30 (intro popover).
  - type: None; the dragon is a template image.
  - colour: The persona's own 0xRRGGBB colour, identical in light and dark. The breath ring is that colour at 0.55 alpha.
  - states: Running: a breath ring swelling from 1.05 to 1.5 while fading from 0.9 to 0 over 1.6 s, forever; Reduce Motion: the ring sits still at 1.05 scale and 0.9 opacity; Finished: an outcome badge on the bottom-right corner; Neither running nor finished: the bare dragon
  - interactions: None; the ring takes no hits
- **UI-HYDRA-033** Everywhere a finished head is shown / Outcome badge on the glyph's bottom-right corner (decoration)
  - geometry: A disc of diameter max(8, size * 0.5), offset by 30 percent of that diameter on both axes, ringed by a stroke of max(1, diameter * 0.12). The symbol is drawn at diameter * 0.55.
  - type: The symbol at .heavy weight.
  - colour: Chrome.success with a checkmark for completed, Chrome.danger with an xmark for failed, Chrome.secondaryText with a filled stop mark otherwise. The symbol is white. The cut-out ring is black at 0.55 in dark and white at 0.95 in light.
  - states: Present only when a status is given AND it is finished; Appears with a scale-and-opacity transition
  - interactions: None
