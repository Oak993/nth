- **UI-CHAT-001** Chat, conversation timeline / Scroll content column (list)
  - geometry: maxWidth 820 lead-aligned; horizontal padding 20; top padding Chrome.contentTopInset = 12 + 32 + 28 = 72; bottom padding 18; minHeight equal to the measured viewport height, top-aligned, with a zero-minimum spacer above the rows; row spacing TimelineMetrics.rowSpacing = 20; during a reshape the layout width is quantized to 12 and capped at 860
  - type: Inherited; every font inside is Font.chat(style, zoom:) at the chat zoom factor
  - colour: No background of its own; the window backdrop shows through
  - states: loading history: renders a transparent zero-content view only; empty and idle: replaced by the new-thread prompt, and the chrome travel is zeroed and the jump button hidden; empty and running: renders the timeline with a working block; short thread: rows rest at the bottom of the pane
  - interactions: scroll (wheel, trackpad, drag); scroll indicators are never shown
- **UI-CHAT-002** Chat, conversation timeline / Show earlier messages pill (button)
  - text, verbatim: `Show 42 earlier messages`
  - geometry: Horizontal padding 14, vertical padding 7, continuous capsule background, centered in the column, 20 points of top padding
  - type: Font.chat(.callout, zoom:)
  - colour: Foreground .secondary; background .quaternary at 0.5 opacity
  - states: hidden when no blocks are hidden; loads a page by itself once at least 10 percent of it is on screen; no separate disabled or pressed styling; the plain button style is used
  - interactions: click: loads one page of 24 older blocks; scroll into view: loads one page automatically
- **UI-CHAT-003** Chat, conversation timeline / Minimap rail column (list)
  - geometry: 30 points wide, full height, top padding equal to the window title bar height; floats over the leading gutter and takes no layout space; ticks centre in (column height minus title bar height); pointer area limited to the tick stack plus half a pitch, never above 44 points from the rail top
  - type: None
  - colour: No background
  - states: hidden when a panel is docked on the leading side; hidden once a measured pane leaves under 900 points of free width after both reserves; shown by default on the unmeasured first frame; hidden when the thread has one or no sent message; expanded (hover or press): base tick opacity rises from 0.25 to 0.4
  - interactions: hover: previews the tick under the pointer and pushes a pointing-hand cursor; press and drag: scrubs the timeline one to one, unanimated; release: one 0.35-second smooth glide to the landing block
