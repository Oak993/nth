- **Preconditions:** A head's name appears in the lead's prose and the timeline holds a mention target for it.
- **Action:** The pointer rests on the name.
- **Expected:** After exactly 250 milliseconds a 300-point card appears above the name, holding the head's 28-point glyph with its running ring and outcome badge, its name at 13-point semibold in ITS OWN persona colour, its task at 12 points on at most three lines, and one status line at 11 points reading exactly 'Working…' with a HORIZONTAL ELLIPSIS U+2026, or 'Done · click to open the chat', 'Failed · click to open the chat' or 'Stopped · click to open the chat' with MIDDLE DOT separators. Hovering the same head again while its card is up does nothing. Moving away cancels a pending card and closes an open one.
- **Side effects:** Clicking the name opens the head's chat through the droppycode-head link
- **Persistence:** None.
- **Failure cases:** The measurement returns a non-positive height: the card is shown at 78 points tall
- **Cancellation:** The pending 250 ms task is cancelled on hide and on a new hover.
- **Windows path:** 250 ms delay, 300 px card, 78 px height fallback. The card must be measured with its width pinned, or the task title wraps after a couple of words in a card that had the room for it.
- **Verify by:** interaction test with a fake clock plus a capture comparison of all four status lines
