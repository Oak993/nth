### PR-GIT-131 - Create pull request pushes first, then titles the request from the last commit's subject and body, with fixed fallbacks

### PR-TERM-001 - The terminal drawer is mounted only while the thread runtime's isTerminalVisible is true, and enters and leaves on a bottom-edge move combined with a fade
