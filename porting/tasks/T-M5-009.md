- **Expected:** The strip is a glass capsule 28 points high, sized to its content, with 3 points of horizontal inner padding and 2 points between segments, in the caller's option order. Each segment is a capsule 22 points high and at least 26 points wide with 9 points of horizontal padding, showing the option's symbol when it has one and otherwise its title verbatim on one line, in the callout text style. The chosen segment is semibold in the primary label colour on a capsule filled with the theme accent at 22 percent; the others are medium weight in Chrome.secondaryText (the label colour at 70 percent) with no fill, and gain a label-colour fill at 8 percent while hovered, animated ease-out over 0.1 seconds. Clicking a segment sets the bound selection to its value inside the same animation. Every segment's accessibility label is its title, also when a symbol is drawn, and the chosen one carries the selected trait. Pressing the strip never drags the window.
