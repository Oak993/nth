- **UI-CHAT-058** Chat, top of the conversation / Pane top veil (label)
  - geometry: Height Chrome.veilHeight = 72, overlaid on the top of the conversation; each colour holds to 28 percent, falls to 0.45 at 62 percent and is clear at the bottom edge
  - type: None
  - colour: Dark: black at 0.14 + 0.18*progress plus the theme glass tint at 0.30. Light: white at 0.30 + 0.26*progress plus the theme glass tint at 0.18. The whole veil's opacity is 0.08 + 0.92*progress
  - states: progress zero: nothing is drawn at all; progress rises over the first 34 points of scroll travel, quantized to steps of 0.04
  - interactions: never hit-testable
- **UI-SIDEBAR-002** Sidebar (all forms) / Thread search field (field)
  - text, verbatim: `Search threads`
  - geometry: HStack spacing 6 inside a capsule; padding horizontal 9; height 30; magnifier glyph then the text field then the optional clear button; the whole row is inset 10 pt horizontally with top padding 14 (12 in the popover)
  - type: placeholder and text .system(size: 13); magnifier .system(size: 12, weight: .semibold)
  - colour: background Capsule(style: .continuous) filled with Chrome.overlay(0.07) = label colour at 0.07; magnifier Chrome.secondaryText at 0.85
  - states: empty (placeholder shown, no clear button); with text (clear button shown); focused (AppKit focus ring behaviour unchanged; not suppressed here, unlike ChromeSearchField)
  - interactions: type to filter; Return selects the first result; Up and Down walk the flattened results and are consumed; Escape empties the field; click the clear button to empty
- **UI-SIDEBAR-003** Sidebar (all forms) / Clear search button (button)
  - geometry: inline at the field's trailing end, inside the capsule's 9 pt padding; no explicit frame, sized by the 12 pt glyph
  - type: glyph .system(size: 12)
  - colour: Chrome.secondaryText at 0.75
  - states: hidden while the field is empty; shown whenever the raw text is non-empty, including whitespace only
  - interactions: click sets the text to the empty string
