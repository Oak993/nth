- **Preconditions:** The composer has a working directory.
- **Action:** The composer appears or its working directory changes.

- **Windows path:** Same shared cache, same limits. RecentCache is the generational cache of port_impacts entry 117, not an LRU.
- **Verify by:** unit test with two composers on one directory counting builds
