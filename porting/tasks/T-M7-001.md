### PR-CHAT-140 - A chat badge is a pill of glyph, title, optional caption and chevron that opens an anchored popover
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:22-52 the pill - an HStack of spacing 8 holding an 18 by 18 glyph, the title at callout medium in Chrome.primaryText at 0.9 with one line and tail truncation, an optional caption at caption size in Chrome.secondaryText with the same truncation, and optionally a chevron.right at caption2 semibold in `Chrome.secondaryText` (:39-43); padded with the pill insets and filled with .tint at 0.1 in a pillRadius continuous rounded rect; the whole pill takes the click, padding included | DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:54-65 needsAttention - a dot-field layer in Chrome.accentNSColor, flipped vertically, covering the top quarter of the badge's height, never hit-testable and hidden from accessibility; the badge is clipped to the pill shape | DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:76-79 the badge is full width lead-aligned with 96 points of trailing padding; its accessibility label is the title and its value is Needs your attention or the empty string
- **Preconditions:** A badge-shaped row is rendered.
- **Action:** The reader clicks it.
- **Expected:** The pill toggles its popover. An attention-needing badge runs the accent dot wave along its top quarter until it is dealt with. A disabled badge does not respond. The screen reader announces the title, and the value Needs your attention only while attention is needed.
- **Persistence:** None.
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** One replicated dot-field control with the nine literals, used in three places, drawing nothing under reduced motion (port_impacts.md 310). Chrome.secondaryText is Chrome.primaryText at opacity 0.7 (ED-062, DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/Chrome.swift:79).
- **Verify by:** capture comparison plus interaction test

### PR-CHAT-141 - A badge popover is measured once, frozen at that size, and hangs from the badge's own rectangle
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:116-152 BadgePopoverCoordinator - behavior applicationDefined, animates true; show measures the hosted content's fitting size, falls back to 460 by 320 when either dimension is not positive, sets the fixed content, and shows relative to the anchor rect with the preferred edge chosen from the anchor view's flippedness | DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:113-115 the doc comment - measured once and frozen, it never resizes off the badge while shown | DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:83-91 badgePopover - the same mechanism for a pill that draws itself, hanging from the view's window rect with the popover's own animation so it grows out of the pill; a SwiftUI popover on the same pill came in from the top edge instead
- **Preconditions:** A badge or a self-drawn pill is opened.
- **Action:** The popover shows.
- **Expected:** The popover opens at its final size, growing out of the pill, and never resizes while shown. Content that cannot be measured falls back to exactly 460 by 320 points.
- **Persistence:** None.
- **Failure cases:** With no anchor rect resolved, show does nothing and the popover never appears.
- **Cancellation:** Closing runs the popover's own animation and the close callback fires once afterwards, whichever way it was closed.
- **Windows path:** A fixed-content popover primitive that pre-measures once, clips top-aligned and never republishes its size, shared by the badge, link, head-hover, attachment and diff popovers (port_impacts.md 308).
- **Verify by:** interaction test that the popover's size does not change after opening

### PR-CHAT-142 - A badge popover closes on Escape in its own or the chat's window, and on a click outside the badge in that window
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:169-187 startMonitors - a local key-down monitor closing on key code 53 when the event's window is the window the popover was shown in or the popover's own window, swallowing that key; and a local monitor for left and right mouse down | DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:189-194 handleMouseDown - events in other windows pass through; a click inside the badge's recorded rect passes through; anything else in that window closes the popover and still passes the event on | DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/ChatBadge.swift:162-167 popoverDidClose - stops the monitors and fires the close callback on the main actor
- **Preconditions:** A badge popover is open.
- **Action:** The reader presses Escape, or clicks elsewhere.
- **Expected:** Escape in the popover or in the chat window it hangs from closes it and is consumed; Escape in another window, such as Settings or a file picker, belongs to that window and is untouched. A click anywhere in the chat window outside the badge's own rect closes the popover, and the click still reaches whatever it landed on.
- **Persistence:** None.
- **Cancellation:** Monitors are removed when the popover closes and when the view disappears.
- **Windows path:** Substitute the local event monitors with an app-scoped message filter: they never see other applications' events (port_impacts.md 308).
- **Verify by:** interaction test for Escape in both windows and for a click-through outside the badge
