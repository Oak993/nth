- **Preconditions:** Process launch, before the first layout.
- **Action:** SidebarLayout is constructed.
- **Expected:** The value stored under the exact key "sidebarWidth" is read as a Double. A missing or non-Double value yields 268. The result is clamped to at least 210 and at most 440, and becomes both the resting width and the current width.

- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** One settings-store indirection for the whole port (port_impacts.md entry 156, C3) holding the same key strings. Read as a floating-point value; the clamp happens on read, not on write.
