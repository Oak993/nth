### PR-COMPOSER-162 - Pulling a linked row more than 60 percent of its height out of its slot unlinks it
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Composer/FollowUpQueueTab.swift:251-259 if the held prompt has a bundle and abs(drag.visualOffset) > (rowHeights[id] ?? 32) * 0.6 { withAnimation(Self.slide) { runtime.unbundleFollowUp(id) }; a levelChange haptic } | porting/port_impacts.md:262 G5 - the 0.6 unlink fraction
- **Preconditions:** A bundled row is being dragged.
- **Action:** The pointer pulls it more than 60 percent of its own height from its slot.
- **Expected:** The row leaves its bundle at once, with a level-change haptic, so a pair with no other rows to swap past can still be broken by drag. Hovering the neighbour's middle again pairs them back on release.
- **Side effects:** Haptic feedback; The queue is saved
- **Persistence:** bundleID cleared and saved.
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** Exactly 0.6 of the measured row height, with the same 32 point fallback.
- **Verify by:** interaction test dragging one of a pair

### PR-COMPOSER-163 - Releasing over a pair target bundles the rows; otherwise the row settles back on a spring
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Composer/FollowUpQueueTab.swift:285-298 dragEnded - with a pair target, bundleFollowUp runs inside .spring(response: 0.36, dampingFraction: 0.78) with a levelChange haptic and the target is cleared; then, if a row was held, the cursor is popped and drag is reset inside .spring(response: 0.32, dampingFraction: 0.8) so the offset animates from wherever the pointer let go back to the row's slot | DroppyCode/App/Runtime/ThreadRuntime.swift:817-830 bundleFollowUp - both prompts take the target's bundle id, or a fresh one; the dragged prompt moves to sit right after the bundle's last prompt | DroppyCode/App/Views/Composer/FollowUpQueueTab.swift:165-178 a local left-mouse-up monitor ends a drag the gesture never reports
- **Preconditions:** A drag is in progress.
- **Action:** The user releases the mouse.
- **Expected:** With a pair target lit, the two prompts join one bundle and the dragged prompt moves after the bundle's last member, on a spring of response 0.36 and damping 0.78, with a level-change haptic. Either way the held row settles back into its slot on a spring of response 0.32 and damping 0.8 and the cursor is popped. A gesture cancelled from under the pointer is caught by the mouse-up monitor.
- **Side effects:** Haptic feedback; The queue is saved
- **Persistence:** bundleID and order saved.
- **Failure cases:** Dropping a row onto itself is rejected by bundleFollowUp's id != target guard.
- **Cancellation:** The monitor is removed when the drag ends and on disappearance.
- **Windows path:** Same springs, same insertion point. The mouse-up monitor becomes an app-scoped message filter or a capture release.
- **Verify by:** interaction test pairing two rows and reordering the bundle

### PR-COMPOSER-173 - The pencil toggles an editor popover whose edit lives on the thread
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Composer/FollowUpQueueTab.swift:455-464 the pencil sets runtime.followUpEdit to a PromptEdit of this prompt, or to nil when it was already open; the button reports its window rect to the editor popover and syncs it | DroppyCode/App/Views/Composer/FollowUpQueueTab.swift:338-342 isEditing is runtime.followUpEdit?.id == prompt.id | DroppyCode/App/Runtime/ThreadRuntime.swift:213-216 followUpEdit lives on the thread so leaving mid-edit and coming back finds the editor open where it was left | DroppyCode/App/Views/Composer/FollowUpQueueTab.swift:504-507 leaving closes the popover and the preview but leaves the edit
