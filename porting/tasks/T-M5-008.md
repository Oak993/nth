## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-WIN-050** Sidebar / Sidebar icon badge and its glyph (label)
  - geometry: Badge box 20 x 20; the glyph inside is fitted to 14 points times its scale, keeping aspect ratio, interpolated at high quality.
  - type: Not type; a symbol or a provider logo.
  - colour: Primary label color at exactly 0.85, monochrome rendering so the glyph takes the surrounding foreground.
  - states: no state variation
  - interactions: none; the row around it takes the click
- **UI-WIN-051** Sidebar / Row accessory icon (button)
  - geometry: 20 x 20 point rectangular hit area around an 11-point glyph.
  - type: 11 points semibold.
  - colour: Secondary label color.
  - states: usually shown only while the row is hovered, which the row passes to the accessory builder
  - interactions: click is handled by whatever wraps it; the icon itself only supplies the shape
- **UI-WIN-052** Settings and welcome pages / Chrome card (container)
  - geometry: Rows stacked with zero spacing, leading-aligned, full width, on a continuous rounded rectangle of radius 16.
  - type: Set by its rows.
  - colour: Primary label color at exactly 0.03.
  - states: rows are built lazily as they scroll into view
  - interactions: none of its own
- **UI-WIN-053** Settings and welcome pages / Chrome section header (label)
  - geometry: Header row with 4 points after the title for an optional accessory, and at least 8 points before an optional trailing control pushed to the far right. Content sits 10 points below the header.
  - type: 13 points semibold.
  - colour: Secondary style.
  - states: with or without the accessory; with or without the trailing control
  - interactions: the accessory and the trailing control carry their own
- **UI-WIN-054** Settings and welcome pages / Chrome row (row)
  - geometry: Leading inset 16, trailing inset 10, vertical padding 11. Text block and control 16 points apart with at least 12 points of space between them. Title and detail 2 points apart.
  - type: Title 13 points; detail 11 points, wrapping to as many lines as needed.
  - colour: Title primary label; detail secondary label.
  - states: detail nil or empty: no second line is drawn or reserved
  - interactions: the row's control carries its own
- **UI-WIN-055** Settings and welcome pages / Chrome row divider (separator)
  - geometry: System hairline, inset 16 points on the leading side only, flush to the trailing edge.
  - type: None.
  - colour: System divider color.
  - states: drawn between consecutive rows only: n rows get n-1 dividers, none before the first or after the last
  - interactions: none
