  - colour: Pill text Chrome.secondaryText on a Capsule filled with Chrome.overlay(0.1).

- **UI-HYDRA-039** Hydra reports popover, single-head header / Head header carrying glyph, name, outcome caption, files line and task (label)
  - geometry: HStack spacing 10 padded 12 horizontally and 10 vertically; the glyph at 24 points; a VStack of spacing 2 whose first row is baseline-aligned with spacing 6.
  - type: Name .system(size: 13, weight: .semibold); outcome caption and files line .system(size: 11) with monospaced digits; task .system(size: 12) on at most three lines.
  - colour: Name Chrome.primaryText; caption Chrome.danger for failed, Chrome.warning for stopped and Chrome.secondaryText for done; files line Chrome.secondaryText; task Chrome.primaryText.
  - states: Done: the caption is exactly "Finished"; Failed: exactly "Failed"; Stopped: exactly "Stopped"; With an effort parsed from the header: " · " and the effort are appended to the caption; With files: "· " then "N file" or "N files" then " · +A −D"; Empty task: the cleaned popover title is shown instead
  - interactions: None
- **UI-HYDRA-040** Hydra reports popover, head card / Name row that toggles the report (button)
  - geometry: HStack spacing 8 at the top of a card padded 12; the glyph at 20 points, a Spacer of at least 8, and a chevron at the trailing edge. The card is backed by a 10-point continuous rounded rectangle when the list is collapsible.
  - type: Name .system(size: 13, weight: .semibold); outcome word and effort .system(size: 11), the effort with monospaced digits on one line; the chevron .system(size: 10, weight: .semibold).
  - colour: The name is drawn in the head's OWN persona colour. The outcome word is Chrome.danger for failed and Chrome.warning for stopped and is absent for done. The effort and the chevron are Chrome.secondaryText. The card background is Chrome.overlay(0.05).
  - states: Collapsed: the chevron points right; Expanded: the chevron is rotated 90 degrees; A head with no report, meaning an empty body or exactly "No report.": the bare name row is shown with no toggle at all; With one head in the batch: there is no name row at all
  - interactions: Click: toggles that head's report open or closed, with NO animation
