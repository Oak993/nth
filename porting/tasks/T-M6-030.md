### PR-LAUNCH-076 - A force kill or crash loses only what the debounced saves had not yet written
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/AppModel.swift:1289-1297 scheduleSave() - the 400 ms debounce is the only routine library persistence | DroppyCode/App/AppModel.swift:129-136 init() - the running-to-interrupted and running-head-to-stopped repairs exist precisely because a previous run may not have terminated cleanly | DroppyCode/Core/Support/HangWatchdog.swift:5-7 - 'A freeze that ends in a force quit leaves nothing behind'
- **Preconditions:** The process is killed without running applicationWillTerminate.
- **Action:** The app is relaunched.
- **Expected:** library.json holds everything that was quiet for 400 ms before the kill; changes newer than that are lost. Thread documents hold whatever their own persistence had written. On relaunch, running turns read as interrupted and running heads read as stopped with a launch-time finishedAt. No recovery prompt, no crash report dialog and no restore-session step is offered.
- **Side effects:** MCP server and terminal children are NOT terminated and may be orphaned, because the termination step never ran
- **Persistence:** Whatever landed before the kill.
- **Failure cases:** Orphaned child processes after a force kill are upstream behavior and are not cleaned up on the next launch.
- **Cancellation:** not applicable
- **Windows path:** Direct port of the recovery behavior. Job Objects with kill-on-close (platform_adaptations PA-0007) would additionally clean up children on a force kill, which is stricter than upstream; that is an improvement to record explicitly rather than assume.
- **Verify by:** restart test: kill the process and assert the relaunch state

### PR-LAUNCH-082 - Forwarding aborts on a vanished source, expands and remembers the target project, and discards the new thread when the continuation will not install
