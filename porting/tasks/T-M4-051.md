### PR-PROVCLI-049 - Claude keeps one stream state per head and takes it out, mutates it and puts it back around each callback
- **Source:** DroppyCode/Services/Providers/ClaudeSession.swift:350-368 - stream_event and assistant cases with the comment at :352-354 | DroppyCode/Services/Providers/ClaudeSession.swift:21-27 - StreamState holds currentMessageID, blockIDs, openText, openThinking | DroppyCode/Services/Providers/ClaudeSession.swift:447-453 - leadSink is lazily made once; agentSink(_:) wraps events in .agentEvent
- **Preconditions:** Streamed content arriving, with or without a parent_tool_use_id.
- **Action:** A stream_event or assistant message is routed.
- **Expected:** With no parent, the lead's single StreamState and the lazily created leadSink are used. With a parent, the head's StreamState is COPIED OUT of the dictionary, mutated, and written BACK after the handler returns, so no state is held open across the callbacks - the sink hands the event on at once and what it reaches can re-enter this session. A head's events are wrapped as .agentEvent(agentID: parent, event).
- **Side effects:** Provider events, possibly re-entrant
- **Persistence:** none
- **Failure cases:** A head whose stream was cleared starts from a fresh StreamState.
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** None at the OS level, but the re-entrancy discipline must survive translation to the Windows event loop; holding a reference into the map across a synchronous callback would be a behavioral change.
- **Verify by:** interaction test with interleaved lead and head streams
