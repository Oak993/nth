  - geometry: A borderless window spanning the union of the source and destination rects inset by -2; one layer animating bounds.size, position and cornerRadius from 6 to 10
  - type: n/a
  - colour: The picked file's thumbnail or Finder icon, aspect fitted, on a clear background
