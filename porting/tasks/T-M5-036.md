## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-HYDRA-035** Chat timeline, hovering a head name in the lead's prose / Head hover card (popover)
  - geometry: 300 points wide, padded 12, leading-aligned; HStack spacing 10 with the 28-point glyph and a VStack of spacing 2. It opens above the name (preferredEdge maxY) after a 250 ms delay. A measured height of zero or less falls back to 78 points.
  - type: Name .system(size: 13, weight: .semibold); task .system(size: 12) on at most three lines; status line .system(size: 11).
  - colour: The name is drawn in the head's OWN persona colour; the task in Chrome.primaryText; the status line in Chrome.secondaryText.
  - states: Running: the glyph carries its breath ring, the status line is exactly "Working…"; Completed: exactly "Done · click to open the chat"; Failed: exactly "Failed · click to open the chat"; Stopped: exactly "Stopped · click to open the chat"; Already shown for the same head: hovering again does nothing
  - interactions: Hover for 250 ms: the card opens; Move away: a pending card is cancelled and an open one is closed; Click the name: opens the head's chat through the droppycode-head link
