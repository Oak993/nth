## Requirements

### PR-CHAT-087 - A right-click popover menu is opened by a secondary click anywhere on a message, at the pointer
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/MessagePopoverMenu.swift:442-466 RightClickPopoverModifier and rightClickPopover - the popover is anchored to a one-by-one rect at the recorded point with the arrow on the top edge, and holds a PopoverMenu of RowActionItems | DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/MessagePopoverMenu.swift:468-521 RightClickCatcher - a transparent overlay whose hitTest returns itself only for a right mouse down or a left mouse down with the control modifier, and nil for every other event, so text selection, links, buttons and drags underneath behave exactly as if it were absent; rightMouseUp is swallowed
- **Preconditions:** A message row is on screen.
- **Action:** The reader right-clicks or control-clicks it.
- **Expected:** A popover menu opens with its arrow pointing at the click point. Ordinary interaction with the content underneath, including selecting text and clicking links, is unaffected because the catcher claims only secondary clicks.
- **Persistence:** None.
- **Cancellation:** Dismissing the popover clears the anchor.
- **Windows path:** Windows has no control-click convention; the trigger is the right mouse button and the context-menu key. The overlay must still be transparent to every other event.
- **Verify by:** interaction test that a left click on a link inside a message still opens the link
