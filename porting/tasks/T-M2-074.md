## Requirements

### PR-LAUNCH-024 - The switch-latency measurement window is one second and produces two distinct log lines
- **Source:** DroppyCode/Core/Support/SwitchLatency.swift:25-36 - on afterWaiting busySince = now and return; on beforeWaiting, 'guard switchStart > 0', span = (now - max(busySince, switchStart)) * 1000, firstSpan set once, busyTotal += span, and when now - switchStart >= 1 it logs and clears switchStart | DroppyCode/Core/Support/SwitchLatency.swift:34 - log.notice("switch to \(label): blocked \(Int(firstSpan ?? 0)) ms, busy \(Int(busyTotal)) ms in 1 s") | DroppyCode/Core/Support/SwitchLatency.swift:50 - log.notice("switch to \(label): blocked \(Int(firstSpan ?? 0)) ms, busy \(Int(busyTotal)) ms before the next switch") | DroppyCode/Core/Support/SwitchLatency.swift:11-12 - Logger(subsystem: "iordv.droppycode", category: "switch") and OSLog(subsystem: "iordv.droppycode", category: .pointsOfInterest)
- **Preconditions:** Development build with the observer installed and a switch in progress.
- **Action:** The main run loop reaches beforeWaiting.
- **Expected:** Spans are computed in milliseconds as (now - max(busySince, switchStart)) * 1000 and reported as truncated integers. Once at least one second has elapsed since the switch, the line "switch to <label>: blocked <n> ms, busy <m> ms in 1 s" is written and the window closes. A second selection inside an open window first writes "switch to <label>: blocked <n> ms, busy <m> ms before the next switch" and then restarts the window. All interpolations are public.
- **Side effects:** os_log notice lines
- **Persistence:** Nothing is persisted.
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** Development-only; not user-visible in a shipped build.
- **Verify by:** unit test with an injected clock over both line shapes
