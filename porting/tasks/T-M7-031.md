- **UI-HYDRA-027** Hydra panel, progress body / Progress bar of staves (chart)
  - geometry: 28 points tall. Each stave is 3 points wide with a 3-point gap, so the pitch is 6 points, with a corner radius of 1.5 points. Stave heights are 45 percent for a lookup, 65 percent for a command, 85 percent for an edit and 100 percent for a reply. Empty slots are drawn at 45 percent height.
  - type: None. ED-059: each text style is set at the point size in upstream's B1 table (subheadline 11; B1:DroppyCode/UI/Common/ChatZoom.swift:44-57), in Inter (OD-0009), and scales only with the app's own zoom (UI/Common/AppZoom.swift); visual size parity stays UNVERIFIED under OD-0001.
  - colour: Edit and reply take the head's persona colour at full strength, command at 0.7 and lookup at 0.4. Empty slots are Chrome.overlay(0.06). A failed head's LAST stave is Chrome.danger and a stopped head's is Chrome.secondaryText. The hovered stave is overdrawn with white at 0.4. The sweep band is a white gradient peaking at 0.55. ED-062: Chrome.secondaryText is Chrome.primaryText at opacity 0.7, as B2 DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/Chrome.swift:79 defines it.
  - states: A new stave rises over 0.45 s with a cubic ease-out; Working, with a sweep slot: a highlight crosses the filled staves for 2.6 s then rests 3.4 s; Without a sweep slot, or under Reduce Motion, or once settled: drawn once per change; Past capacity: the oldest staves slide off the left
  - interactions: Hover: the stave under the pointer lifts toward white and a card above the bar names its step; Move off: the card fades out over 0.15 s
