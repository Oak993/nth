### PR-PROVCLI-187 - Copilot finishes a turn by clearing five caches, cancelling prompts and choosing one of three statuses
- **Source:** DroppyCode/Services/Providers/CopilotSession.swift:695-715 - finishTurn(aborted:) with its comment
- **Preconditions:** A turn is active.
- **Action:** session.idle arrives, or a command reply completes the turn.
- **Expected:** With no active turn the call returns at once. Otherwise turnActive becomes false and toolNames, declinedTools, taskBriefs and shownReasoning are all cleared, because every call of the turn has had its result. cancelPendingRequests then answers and withdraws anything still pending. The stored turn error is taken and cleared. Then: aborted OR interruptRequested -> .turnCompleted(.interrupted, nil); else a stored error -> .turnCompleted(.failed, error); else .turnCompleted(.completed, nil). interruptRequested is reset last. Note that streamedMessages is NOT cleared here, only in beginTurn.
- **Side effects:** Provider events; replies to pending requests
- **Persistence:** none
- **Failure cases:** An aborted turn with a stored error reports interrupted, not failed.
- **Cancellation:** An abort or an interrupt both produce .interrupted.
- **Windows path:** None.
- **Verify by:** interaction test over a clean turn, an errored turn and an aborted turn
