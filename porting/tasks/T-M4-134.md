### PR-PROVREG-193 - Spending a Codex reset resolves the CLI, calls the app-server, then re-reads the limits in the same pass

- **Cancellation:** not cancellable once the spend is sent.
- **Windows path:** Same order and the same non-fatal re-read.
- **Verify by:** contract fixture against a scripted app-server
