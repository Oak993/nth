## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-THEME-047** Chat, a streaming reply / Stream veil over newly arrived text (decoration)
  - geometry: No geometry of its own: the text is already laid out in its final position and nothing moves or resizes.
  - type: Unchanged.
  - colour: Arriving characters are drawn in the primary colour at the chunk's alpha; when dimmed the base is the primary colour at 70 percent opacity, to which the chunk's alpha is applied. A link run is drawn in the theme accent at that alpha.
  - states: fade duration is three times an exponential moving average of the flush gaps, clamped to 0.12 and 0.40 s; three or more chunks in flight speed each other up by 30 percent a chunk, retroactively; opacity follows 1 minus (1 minus progress) to the power 1.6; holds its last frame while the timeline is scrolling; text already on screen when a veil attaches never fades
  - interactions: not interactive
