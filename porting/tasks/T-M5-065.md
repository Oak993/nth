## Requirements

### PR-THEME-082 - The settle chime is a 0.32 second descending step at volume 0.18 that restarts from the top on every request
- **Source:** DroppyCode/UI/Sound/SettleChime.swift:9-10 `private static let volume: Float = 0.18` with the comment "Barely there: a tick under the sidebar's motion, not a notification." | DroppyCode/UI/Sound/SettleChime.swift:12-16 built once from `ToneSynth.wave(notes: notes, partials: partials, duration: 0.32)` | DroppyCode/UI/Sound/SettleChime.swift:17-21 `static func play() { sound?.stop(); sound?.play() }` with the comment "A replay from the top, so settling two threads in a row ticks twice." | DroppyCode/UI/Sound/SettleChime.swift:23-27 notes: frequency 659.26 onset 0 level 0.7; frequency 523.25 onset 0.075 level 1
- **Preconditions:** A thread has settled and the settle sound setting is on.
- **Action:** The settle chime is asked to play, possibly twice in quick succession.
- **Expected:** A 0.32 second tone plays at volume 0.18: a note at 659.26 Hz at level 0.7 from time 0, followed at 0.075 seconds by a note at 523.25 Hz at level 1, built from three partials in this order - fundamental at level 1 decaying with a 0.075 second constant, the third harmonic at 0.16 decaying at 0.02 (the woody knock), and the octave at 0.08 decaying at 0.03. A second request stops the sound and replays from the beginning, so two settles in a row tick twice. This is the opposite policy to the finish chime.
- **Side effects:** Stops any in-flight playback of the same sound, then plays audio
- **Persistence:** not persisted
- **Failure cases:** The sound cannot be created: play() is a silent no-op.
- **Cancellation:** A later play() cancels the current one by restarting it.
- **Windows path:** Platform boundary: reproduce stop-then-play on one shared voice, not a mix of two overlapping voices. The declared partial order (3rd harmonic before the octave) does not change the sum but is recorded for fidelity of the constant table.
- **Verify by:** unit: constants; interaction test: two settles in quick succession are audibly two ticks
