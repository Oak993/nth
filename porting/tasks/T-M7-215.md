### PR-TERM-014 - Dragging the strip sets a live height of origin plus upward travel, clamped to 140...760, without writing settings
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Terminal/TerminalPanel.swift:87-90 - onChange computes origin as dragOrigin ?? model.settings.terminalHeight and sets dragHeight = min(760, max(140, origin + delta)) | DroppyCode/App/Views/Terminal/TerminalPanel.swift:127 - `onChange: { onChange($0.height) }`, taking only the height component of DragHandle's reported travel | DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/DragHandle.swift:210-220 mouseDragged - once dragging (Terminal's slop 0 means it always is by the time a move arrives), `onChange?(travel)` on every move | DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/DragHandle.swift:259-266 travel(to:) for `.vertical` - `CGSize(width: 0, height: point.y - pressOrigin.y)`

- **Action:** The pointer moves.
- **Expected:** delta is the pointer's travel since the press in window coordinates with y increasing upward (event.locationInWindow.y minus the press's y, exactly as at B2), so dragging UP gives a positive delta and grows the drawer. dragHeight becomes min(760, max(140, origin + delta)), clamped on every frame. - **Verify by:** unit test on the clamp plus an interaction test on the direction
