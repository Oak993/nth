### PR-HYDRA-188 - The bar caches its staves by row identity plus tool status and title, so a re-render with no change does not walk the timeline again
- **Source:** DroppyCode/App/Views/Chat/HydraProgressBar.swift:61-120 StaveCache - the key is the row's object identity plus, for a tool row, its status and its title, because a status flip rewrites the step's verb and the title streams in after the row arrives; resolve returns the cached triple when the capacity is unchanged and the entries match; matches walks the stored keys in place without allocating and exits at the first row that differs
- **Preconditions:** The bar re-renders, which happens once per slot as the pointer crosses the staves and on every clock tick.
- **Action:** The staves are resolved.
- **Expected:** A cache hit skips the walk over every entry entirely. The key is the row's identity, its tool status and its tool title; a plain row keys on identity alone. The comparison is done in place against the stored keys, not against a freshly built array, because the pointer crossing the staves re-evaluates this body once per slot and each pass would otherwise allocate one key per row of the head's whole timeline only to throw it away.
- **Persistence:** None.
- **Failure cases:** The capacity changed: the cache misses and everything is recomputed
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** Memoise on the same three-part key; this is a performance contract.
- **Verify by:** unit asserting no recomputation on an unchanged render and recomputation on a status flip
