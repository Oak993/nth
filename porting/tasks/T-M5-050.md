### PR-THEME-149 - A prose run renders several blocks as one native text object with explicit paragraph styles, and drops code, tables and rules
- **Source:** DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:346-368 joinedSources() and collect(): paragraph and heading texts, list item texts and their children, and quote children are gathered and joined with newlines; code, tables and rules contribute nothing | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:370-377 attributed(): every block is appended with depth 0, indent 0, spacing 12 | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:384-392 a paragraph gets a style with the current indent as both first-line and head indent and a paragraph spacing of the caller's spacing | DroppyCode/UI/Markdown/ProseRunView.swift:393-417 a heading is rendered at `(pointSize * 1.35).rounded()` for level 1, `* 1.2` for level 2, `* 1.08` for level 3 and the base size otherwise; every run that is neither bold nor monospaced is set to the semibold face at that size; its style has a spacing before of 6 for levels 1 and 2 and 2 otherwise, and a paragraph spacing of 12

Quoted content is indented a further 14 points and rendered dimmed, with no bar. Blocks are separated by a newline.
