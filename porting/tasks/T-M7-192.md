Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-SIDEBAR-045** Floating sidebar panel over the chat / Panel container (popover)
  - geometry: default 300x560, minimum 240x320, capped at the chat area less 2 * 12 pt margin; default origin (12, 72); 22 pt continuous corner radius; the sidebar inside is inset 44 pt from the top
  - type: n/a
  - colour: regular glass effect washed with Chrome.glassTint at 0.22 (dark) or 0.16 (light); behind it a plate of black at 0.3 (dark) or white at 0.34 (light) casting a black shadow at 1.0 (dark) or 0.65 (light), radius 28, y 10
  - states: resting (origin changes animate with Chrome.panelSlide); being dragged (no animation on the origin); being resized
  - interactions: drag the strip to move; drag the bottom-right grip to resize
- **UI-SIDEBAR-046** Floating sidebar panel / Panel title capsule (label)
  - text, verbatim: `Threads`
  - geometry: padding horizontal 12 (capsuleHorizontalPadding), content height 28 (capsuleContentHeight), padding vertical 2 (capsuleVerticalPadding), fixedSize; inset top 12 (chromeTopPadding), leading 14 (chromeHorizontalPadding)
  - type: .system(size: 12.5, weight: .medium)
  - colour: Chrome.primaryText at 0.92 on a chrome glass capsule
  - states: static
  - interactions: a drag handle is overlaid on it, so pressing the title drags the panel
- **UI-SIDEBAR-047** Floating sidebar panel / Panel drag handle (button)
  - geometry: fills the remaining strip width at the full 44 pt strip height
  - type: n/a
  - colour: transparent
  - states: idle; dragging (the panel follows with no animation)
  - interactions: press and drag to move the panel, clamped inside the area with a 12 pt margin; the resting origin is persisted on release
- **UI-SIDEBAR-048** Floating sidebar panel / Panel close button (button)
  - geometry: ChromeCircleButton; inset top 12, trailing 14
  - type: chrome circle button defaults
  - colour: chrome circle button defaults
  - states: present only when canClose is true, which is false in the panel-only sidebar mode
  - interactions: click puts the list back into the column
- **UI-SIDEBAR-049** Floating sidebar panel / Panel resize grip (button)
  - geometry: 16x16 at the panel's bottom-trailing corner
  - type: n/a
  - colour: transparent
  - states: idle; dragging
  - interactions: press and drag to resize; the cursor is a bottom-right frame resize cursor both on hover and while dragging; the size is written to settings on every drag tick
