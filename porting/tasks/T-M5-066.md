## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-MCP-092** Transcript tool call / MCP tool glyph (icon)
  - text, verbatim: `<server name> tool`
  - geometry: square at the caller's size, default 20; a breathing ring is drawn behind it at the same size with line width 1.5 while the call is running; the status badge is a circle of diameter max(8, size * 0.5) offset by 0.3 of its diameter on both axes, with a border of max(1, diameter * 0.12)
  - type: the badge symbol is system at diameter * 0.55, heavy weight
  - colour: the glyph is Chrome.primaryText for a monochrome entry and the entry's brand colour otherwise; the running ring is the entry's colour at opacity 0.55; a failed badge is Chrome.danger and a declined badge is Chrome.warning, both with a white symbol; the badge border is black at opacity 0.55 in dark mode and white at opacity 0.95 in light mode
  - states: running: the breathing ring is drawn behind the glyph and does not take hit tests; failed: an xmark badge in the danger colour at the bottom trailing corner; declined: a hand.raised.slash badge in the warning colour; every other status: no badge
  - interactions: none; it is decoration on a tool call row
