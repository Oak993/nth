## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-HYDRA-023** Popped-out head panel, top strip / Put-back button (button)
  - geometry: A chrome circle button, padded 12 at the top and 14 at the trailing edge; it is the only button in this strip.
  - type: The arrow.down.left symbol at the chrome circle button's own icon size.
  - colour: The chrome circle button's own colours.
  - states: Present only in a popped-out panel, where the heads capsule is hidden
  - interactions: Click: puts the head back in the team panel; Hover: tooltip exactly "Put {head display name} back in the team panel", falling back to "Put the head back in the team panel" when there is no name
- **UI-HYDRA-024** Hydra panel, native head / Head footer in place of a chat box (label)
  - geometry: HStack spacing 8; padded 14 horizontally and 10 vertically; backed by a 16-point continuous rounded rectangle; then padded 20 horizontally and 14 at the bottom.
  - type: Status text .system(size: 12); the step count .system(size: 11) with monospaced digits; the outcome symbol at .system(size: 12).
  - colour: Fill Chrome.panelControlFill(isDark:), which is Chrome.glassTint mixed toward white in dark by 0.12 or toward black in light by 0.04, at 0.55 opacity in dark and 0.42 in light. Text in Chrome.secondaryText. The outcome mark is Chrome.success for completed, Chrome.danger for failed and Chrome.secondaryText for stopped.
  - states: Running: a MiniSpinner and the live activity truncated to 80 characters, or exactly "Working"; Completed: a filled checkmark circle and "Done" plus elapsed, steps, landing words and the summary; Failed: a filled xmark circle and "Failed" plus the summary; Stopped: a filled stop circle and exactly "Stopped"; No tool ran: the step count is omitted
  - interactions: None; it is read-only, because a native head lives inside the lead's session and cannot be steered
- **UI-HYDRA-025** Hydra panel, Droppy-run head / The head's own chat box (field)
  - geometry: A full ComposerArea inset at the bottom of the panel, with a JumpToLatestButton overlaid at its top.
  - type: The composer's own.
  - colour: The composer's own.
  - states: Present only when the head's kind is droppy; compactModelChip is on, focus is NOT taken on appear, and the changes strip is hidden
  - interactions: Type and send: steers the head on with another turn, which resets its status to running and makes it report again when that turn ends
- **UI-HYDRA-026** Hydra panel, on a head finishing / Finish wave across the whole panel (decoration)
  - geometry: A DotFieldSweep filling the panel, clipped to the panel's 22-point continuous rounded rectangle.
  - type: None.
  - colour: Tinted with the finishing head's own persona colour, falling back to roster index 0 when there is none.
  - states: Plays for exactly 2.0 seconds after a head on stage goes from running to finished; Fades in and out with Chrome.panelSlide so it never pops onto the stage; A new hold for the same head plays the wave again
  - interactions: None
- **UI-HYDRA-073** Any floating panel / Panel surface (decoration)
  - geometry: A 22-point continuous rounded rectangle at the panel's computed size. The panel is clipped to that shape. A separate scrim of the same shape sits behind it.
  - type: None.
  - colour: One regular glass surface washed with Chrome.glassTint at 0.22 in dark and 0.16 in light. The scrim is black at 0.3 in dark and white at 0.34 in light. Its shadow is black at 1.0 in dark and 0.65 in light, radius 28, y offset 10.
  - states: No hairline border at any time, because glass supplies its own edge
  - interactions: None
