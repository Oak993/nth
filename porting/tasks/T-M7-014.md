## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-HYDRA-001** Chat chrome row / Hydra mark button, the first control in the chrome row (button)
  - geometry: A 32 by 32 frame (Chrome.capsuleHeight = capsuleContentHeight 28 + capsuleVerticalPadding 2 * 2), hit-shaped as a circle, drawn on a chrome glass circle. The mark image inside is 18 by 18.
  - type: None; the control is an image.
  - colour: The mark is Chrome.primaryText (labelColor) at full opacity when Hydra is on for the chat or the pointer is over it, and at 0.6 otherwise. The glass circle takes Chrome.glassTint.
  - states: Off for the chat: mark at 0.6 opacity, no charge ring; On: mark at full opacity, charge ring and glow present; Hovering: mark at full opacity, animated with Chrome.hover (ease-out 0.1 s); Switching on or off: the charge scales from 0.6 and fades with Chrome.panelSlide
  - interactions: Left click: intro popover if unread, else idle popover if the chat has no heads, else show or hide the team panel; Right click or Control-click: the per-chat switch popover, consumed so nothing else sees the event; Hover: tooltip assembled from up to four parts joined by " · "
- **UI-HYDRA-002** Chat chrome row / Working-heads badge above the mark's top-right corner (label)
  - geometry: Minimum 14 by 14, padded 4 horizontally and 1 vertically, drawn in a Capsule, offset x +3 and y -3 from the button's top-trailing corner.
  - type: The count is .system(.caption2, weight: .bold) with monospaced digits: 10 points at the macOS caption2 text-style size (text-style point sizes per dd2b86ef DroppyCode/UI/Common/ChatZoom.swift:45-57 chatBaseSize (B1; the file is deleted at B2, where DroppyCode/UI/Common/ChatFont.swift:6-10 returns Font.system(style) with no zoom): title2 17, title3 15, body 13, callout 12, subheadline 11, footnote 10, caption2 10). The sending state shows a MiniSpinner at cellSize 2.4 instead.
  - colour: Background Chrome.glassTint while sending and Chrome.accent while showing a count; the count text is pure white.
  - states: Absent: no head running, not sending and not delegating; Sending or delegating: mini spinner on the glass tint; Working: the count in white on the accent; Appearing and disappearing: scale combined with opacity; Count change: spring response 0.3, damping 0.7
  - interactions: Click: the badge is part of the button, so tapping it taps the button; Hover: tooltip exactly "1 head working · Show or hide the Hydra panel" or "N heads working · Show or hide the Hydra panel"
- **UI-HYDRA-003** Chat chrome row, Hydra charge / Charge ring and glow around the mark (decoration)
  - geometry: The glow is a Circle inset 2 points from the 32-point frame with a radial gradient running from radius 2 to radius 15 from the centre. The ring is a Circle inset 1 point with a 2-point stroked border; its mask is an ellipse inset by half the line width.
  - type: None.
  - colour: The glow is Chrome.accent at 0.46 alpha fading to Chrome.accent at 0 alpha. The ring is an angular gradient over the FIRST SIX roster colours (0xFF8A3D, 0x3D8BFF, 0x34C46A, 0xA35BE0, 0xF25C9A, 0x2BB5B0) plus the first again to close it. The conic layer version reverses that order because its angle grows counter-clockwise on a y-up layer.
  - states: Idle: ring still at 0.85 opacity, glow still at 0.6 opacity; Working: the ring turns once every 4 s linearly and both breathe to full opacity and back over 1.4 s, ease-in-ease-out; Reduce Motion: the still form, even while working
  - interactions: None; the charge never takes hits
