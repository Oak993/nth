## UI contract records

Exact values. Do not round, restyle or substitute. Where a value is macOS-delegated, say so.

- **UI-STORE-005** Attachment chip, attachment strip, downloads popover, attachment preview / Rendered thumbnail of an image attachment (image)
  - geometry: The decoded bitmap’s longest edge is max(1, ceil(pointSize * 2)) pixels; with fillingSquare that is multiplied by the aspect ratio and clamped to 2048. The point size itself is chosen by the calling view, which belongs to the composer and timeline areas.
  - type: not applicable
  - colour: the image’s own pixels; the EXIF orientation transform is applied at decode
  - states: loading: nothing is drawn until the decode lands, and the drawing view fades it in; already decoded: found synchronously in ThumbnailMemory and drawn in the same layout pass with no fade; missing or unreadable file: the decode returns nil and no image is drawn; caller cancelled: nil is returned to that caller while the shared decode still completes for the others
  - interactions: none of its own; the surrounding chip owns click and hover
