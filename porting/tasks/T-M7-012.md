### PR-HYDRA-160 - The delegation-block scan is cached per row: a 32-entry cache for finished replies and an incremental scan with 32 bytes of overlap for streaming ones

- **Preconditions:** A turn is streaming and the button re-renders on every token.
- **Action:** The button tests each reply of the turn for a delegation block.

- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** Measure the overlap in UTF-8 bytes, matching the source, not in UTF-16 units.
- **Verify by:** unit feeding a fence split across two flushes and a reply that shrinks
