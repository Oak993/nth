### PR-HYDRA-196 - HydraPairMark draws the lead's provider mark, an arrow and the heads' provider mark at fixed proportions of the lead size
- **Source:** DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/HydraPairMark.swift:5-23 struct HydraPairMark - leadSize defaults to 16; the HStack spacing is (leadSize * 0.25).rounded(); the lead's ProviderIcon is at leadSize in Chrome.primaryText; the arrow is Image(systemName: "arrow.right") at (leadSize * 0.5).rounded() semibold in Chrome.secondaryText; the heads' ProviderIcon is at (leadSize * 0.75).rounded() in Chrome.primaryText at 0.8; the whole is one accessibility element labelled "{lead display name} lead, {heads display name} heads"
- **Preconditions:** A pair is drawn anywhere a pair is shown.
- **Action:** The mark renders.
- **Expected:** At the default lead size of 16 points: spacing 4, lead mark 16 points at full primary text, arrow 8 points semibold in secondary text, heads mark 12 points at 80 percent primary text. Every derived value is rounded to the nearest whole point. The mark is one accessibility element whose label is exactly '{lead provider display name} lead, {heads provider display name} heads'. Every place a pair is drawn uses this one view, so the proportions are the same everywhere.
- **Persistence:** None.
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** Round the derived sizes; do not use fractional pixel sizes. ED-062: Chrome.secondaryText is Chrome.primaryText at opacity 0.7, as B2 DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/Chrome.swift:79 defines it.
- **Verify by:** capture comparison at lead sizes 16 and 24
