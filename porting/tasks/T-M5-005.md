### PR-WIN-212 - The content below a chrome row starts 72 points down, which is numerically but not semantically the veil's height
- **Source:** DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/Chrome.swift:39-41 contentBreathBelowChrome = 28, `static var contentTopInset: CGFloat { chromeTopPadding + capsuleHeight + contentBreathBelowChrome }`, veilHeight = 72 | porting/extracts/chrome-design-tokens.md section 3: 'contentTopInset (72) and veilHeight (72) are numerically equal but are independent declarations; do not fold them into one constant.'
- **Preconditions:** A pane with a sticky chrome row draws.
- **Action:** Its content is inset from the top.
- **Expected:** The content begins 12 + 32 + 28 = 72 points below the pane's top edge. The veil is also 72 points tall, but through an unrelated literal.
- **Persistence:** nothing persisted
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** Keep the two as separate constants. Folding them means a change to one silently moves the other.
- **Verify by:** unit assertion that both resolve to 72 through different expressions

### PR-WIN-213 - A field capsule takes the same glass without the press response, because the bounce and the caret landing together made it flicker
- **Source:** DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/Chrome.swift:131-137 `/// The capsule for a field rather than a button: the same glass, without the press response. A field is clicked to place the caret, and interactive glass answered the click with its press bounce at the same moment the field took focus, so the capsule flickered on every tap.` with `func chromeGlassFieldCapsule() -> some View { modifier(ChromeGlassSurface(shape: .capsule, isInteractive: false)) }` | DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/Chrome.swift:154-155 `/// Whether the glass answers a press; off for a field that takes the caret instead.` with `var isInteractive = true` | DroppyCode/UI/Chrome/Chrome.swift:177-181 `} else if isInteractive { content.glassEffect(.regular.tint(Chrome.glassTint.opacity(0.3)).interactive(), in: shape) } else { content.glassEffect(.regular.tint(Chrome.glassTint.opacity(0.3)), in: shape) }`
- **Preconditions:** A chrome capsule holds a text field rather than a button, and it is not inside a glass panel.
- **Action:** The user clicks the field to place the caret.
- **Expected:** The field capsule draws the identical glass material with the identical theme tint at 0.3 opacity as a button capsule, but without the interactive press response. Clicking it places the caret and the capsule does not bounce. The reason is recorded in the source and is observable: with the press response on, the bounce fired at the same moment the field took focus and the capsule flickered on every tap. This distinction applies only off a glass panel; on a glass panel the on-panel branch is taken first and a field gets the same hover brightening a button does (PR-WIN-214).
- **Persistence:** nothing persisted
- **Cancellation:** not cancellable
- **Windows path:** If the Windows chrome control has no press animation at all, this requirement is satisfied trivially, but the reverse is the failure to avoid: giving every capsule a press animation for consistency reintroduces exactly the flicker the source removed. Glass itself is subject to UNK-0004.
- **Verify by:** capture comparison of a click on a field capsule and on a button capsule, frame by frame through the press
- **Depends on UNK-0004**, which is unresolved. Implement only the part the source settles; flag the rest.
