### PR-PROVAPI-121 - A dropped connection is retried twice, after one second and then two, and only for three transport codes

- **Preconditions:** A request failed at the transport level.
- **Action:** The round decides what to do.
- **Expected:** The identical request is sent again at most twice, waiting one second before the first retry and two before the second. A rejected key, a validation error or a stop is never retried. - **Windows path:** Map to the equivalent Windows network error conditions by code, not by message text.
- **Verify by:** integration with a fault-injecting transport
