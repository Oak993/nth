Marrow boot splash

The Comet mark, large, at start-up. Particles in the arms' own colours spiral in and settle into the mark, and the core's pulse ignites the first turn. From then on the core pulses once each time a turn comes to rest, as if each pulse drives the next turn. It stays in that loop until the app is ready, then hands over to the main window.

  1. 0 to 1.45 s, gather. 216 particles start around the edge of the splash and spiral inward on eased paths, arm by arm. Each arm fades in as its particles land, and the particles melt into it.
  2. 1.2 to 1.45 s, ignite. The core's particles land and the core draws in for a moment. At 1.45 s it pulses: a flash and one ring of light, and that pulse sets the first turn going. The tile rises in behind from 94 % scale.
  3. From 1.45 s, running. One eased turn every 2.4 s, from rest to rest. Each time the turn comes to a stop the core draws in and pulses once, releasing one ring, and the pulse starts the next turn. Trails show only at speed.
  4. Ready. Once the app is ready and the gather has finished, the mark grows 6 % and fades over 0.45 s while the main window appears.
