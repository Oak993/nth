Marrow loading mark, round 2

Three premium treatments of your finalized icon. Each layers several coordinated motions (light, depth, glow, timing) instead of one spin. Shown on the dark and light themes at 144 px, and at 64, 40 and 24 px with a real loading label.

A. Tide

The arms breathe out from their middles and back, one after another, while the mark turns a third with an eased settle. The core gathers light as the arms draw in, and a soft highlight sweeps the glass every few seconds.

B. Comet

One full turn with a living speed, from rest to rest. Motion trails appear only while the arms move fastest. Each time the turn comes to a stop, the core draws in and pulses once, releasing one ring of light, and that pulse sets the next turn going.

C. Glass

The tile floats in depth. The arms and the core sit on their own layers and part in parallax as it tilts, the highlight answers the tilt, and the arms turn slowly underneath.
