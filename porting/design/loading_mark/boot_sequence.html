Marrow boot sequence

Particles form the mark, the core's pulse sets one turn going, the turn comes to rest, and the mark breaks back into particles that fly out and build the main window: its edge first, then its contents.

  1. 0 to 1.45 s, gather. 216 particles in the arms' colours spiral in from around the mark and form it; the tile rises in behind.
  2. 1.45 s, pulse. The core draws in, flashes and releases one ring of light, and that pulse starts the turn.
  3. 1.45 to 3.85 s, one turn. One eased turn, trails only at speed, ending at rest. If the app is not ready yet, the core pulses again at rest and another turn follows, until it is.
  4. 3.95 s, disintegrate. At rest, the mark dissolves into its particles, which lift off, burst outward a little, and sweep out along curved paths without crossing.
  5. About 4.1 to 5.4 s, the window forms. The first particles trace the window's edge, which lights up as they land; the rest settle into the sidebar, the conversation and the composer, and the window's contents fade in under them as they melt away.
