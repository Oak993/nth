The original 22.07% and 35.52% steps are now legitimately accepted by OD-0126. Frame 3 exceeds the exact oracle by **3.8861765417e-7 of travel**: the rig's `TimeSpan.FromMilliseconds(1000/60)` increments are quantized to 16.6666 ms, whereas the source oracle is sampled at nominal `i * 1000/60` ms. The falling side of the curve consequently has a slightly larger actual step at frame 3. This is a numerical sampling discrepancy, not visible stutter. Neither clock nor sampling fixture was changed.

Independent review also evaluated source samples at the actual quantized cadence: that removes the larger timing difference, but the unchanged 40-iteration oracle underestimates the port's higher-precision step at frames 1/2 by about 1.17e-12/1.17e-13, so a strict comparison still fails. No epsilon or rounding was silently added to bypass either discrepancy. Resolving that requires an explicit sampling/numerical comparison rule compatible with the owner's no-margin instruction; this branch does not invent one.
