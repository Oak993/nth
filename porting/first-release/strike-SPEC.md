## 1. Coordinates and clocks

All of these are **SOURCE FACT**.

- **Page time `t`** (s). The track's own clock. In the port this is the TrackEffect time: seconds since the track
  appeared, advanced on the render thread (ED-237 and ED-248 review 2). The page's tick: P:1575-1609.
- **Strike time `T = t + off`.** `off` is the track's offset, set by the random clean starts (section 10). Everything
  about strikes is a function of T (P:772, E:209). The knob's idle wash is the exception: it breathes on t (P:374-378).
- **Bubble time `B(t) = clockB(t)`.** The fast ramp integrated at rate 1 + 0.75 f(t) (E:104, E:969). Bubbles,
  contacts and bursts are keyed by t and B, never by T. That is why a re-entry jump leaves them untouched (P:1326-1328).
- **Geometry `g`** (P:752-753), in CSS px of the tube canvas, origin at the tube's top-left:
  - `w`: the whole tube width, not the fill;
  - `h`: the tube height; `cy = h / 2`; the cap end `xs = 0.32 h`;
  - `ex`: where the bolt leaves the knob, `round((knobCentreX - 2) * 4) / 4` (P:1083, P:1591);
  - `kr`: the knob radius, `knob / 2`;
  - `k = clamp(h / 40, 0.62, 1)`.
- **The canvases are clipped by the liquid's fill** (`.liqwrap`: overflow hidden, width = knob centre + kr, P:87,
  P:925).
- **The app's geometry** (ENGINEERING DECISION from OD-0090 and ED-252):
  - the track is 298 x 34 (the like-for-like evidence of ED-252), so h = 34, k = 0.85, cy = 17, xs = 10.88;
  - the knob is 48 px, so kr = 24;
  - ex = 272 at Max (knob centre 274) and 188.75 at High of four stops.
  The vectors use these as `appMax` and `appHigh`, beside the page's `pageBig` (520 x 40, knob 56, ex 335.25) and
  `pageSmall` (300 x 26, knob 36, ex 280).
- **The app's tube is on the large side of the plume's threshold.** `big = h >= 34` (P:428), so the app draws 6
  filaments and two branch levels (as the 40 px tube does), not the 26 px tube's 4 and one. SOURCE FACT of the code.
  OD-0093's prose speaks only of the page's two tubes.

### 2.3 Threads and the frame clock (ED-237, ED-248 review 2)

The track's frames come at the compositor's ticks on the render thread. `LiquidTrack.Live` resolves the frame where it
is drawn, under the lock on the track's `LiquidLayers`. The strike unit keeps all of its per-frame state on that thread
under that lock:

- the StrikeClock;
- the plan ring;
- the plume resolution;
- the contacts and bursts;
- the bubble lanes;
- the knob's signal inputs.

**The UI thread only posts intents** (fast on or off with its time, a model change, reduce motion, the knob's x, the
look). It posts each as an immutable record read with `Volatile.Read`, as `Scene` is today. Main records nothing per
frame (ED-248 review 2), and the strike unit adds no per-frame work on main.

**The knob's operation** (JewelKnobOperation, render thread) reads the same StrikeClock and plan ring at the same T, so
the knob and the tube can never disagree about a strike (OD-0084: the orb charges first, then the bolt leaves it).

**Pacing** is ED-237 / ED-232: 60 updates a second, counted in whole refreshes on refresh-numbering sources. Do not
copy the page's own `frameGate` (E:56-67). The strike maths is a pure function of T, the bubbles are a function of t,
and only the leader steps and the 30 Hz shimmer are stepped, as written.

### 2.4 Inputs that change at run time (OD-0076: none of them may cut)

- **The knob moves.** ex changes, and the plan ring's geometry is dropped and rebuilt for the strikes in view (P:731).
  Timing is kept: it is geometry-independent. The strike in flight follows the knob.
- **The model changes.** These crossfade over 0.6 s smoothstep (0.2 s under reduce motion; P:949-950, P:1263-1284):
  - the tint (outer, inner, wash, deep, by rounded channels);
  - the ink amount (linear);
  - inkc and the ink palette (rounded channels, `mixTint2`, P:838-841).

  The slider tube also re-enters the sequence when quiet (10.4).
- **Fast mode toggles.** The 0.5 s ramp (7.1).
- **Reduce motion toggles.** The still (11).

int arithmetic: it is the same modulo 2^32 for every value here (all intermediates stay below 2^53 in JS).

(banker's). Alpha strings use `toFixed(3)`: use `Strike.Fixed3`.

Screen is associative and commutative in premultiplied form (`s + d - s d` per channel). So zapb and zap screened

The glass pass (ED-248's `LitEffect`) samples the liquid refracted (bubbles inside, as v22), then `over`, then `screen`,

the refracted liquid, under it. It is inherited from ED-248/ED-252 and is minor, because every bubble a lit line
touches is gone before it is drawn. Recorded for the owner's eye check, not changed here.

### 6.5 The frame order (P:730-781, then process and drawBubbles)

1. If ex changed, drop the plan ring's geometry (keep timing).
2. Compute g. F.ink comes from the live tint, op from f(t), and lo. `hw = 0.6 + 0.6 F.ink` (K-142).
3. `T = t + off` (still: K-173).
4. If op > 0.002, run the storm at T:
   1. each bolt's washes go to the light ops;
   2. each plume queues its mask strokes and adds its lit lines;
   3. the idle wash;
   4. the spark (if not busy): light glow, lock masks and lit;
