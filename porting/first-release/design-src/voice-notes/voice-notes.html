The error notes: which words?

Already pickedEach note says what happened, plus one link to the place that fixes it (OD-0099). Where and how a note shows is the locked one’s (V-14.6): with an empty draft it replaces the placeholder, with its warning mark; with text in the draft it shows in the composer’s notice line above your text, so your words are never hidden.

Four word sets for the eleven notes. Choose a set, a note and the draft below to see it in the composer; the full list of every note in every set is under the cards. The links are underlined and each one says where it goes.

How long a note stays

The locked note stays 5.2 s, then the placeholder (or your text) comes back (V-14.6). The others have no time yet; the lead proposes the same 5.2 s (ED). The spoken version of each note (V-14.5 has one for the blocked note) is written in VT-17 from the picked words.

To confirm: the download note

An orb click during the first download already shows the download in the pill (“Getting voice ready”, the welcome-voice pick, VT-15). So “Model still downloading” is for when that pill is not shown. Whether both show is for the lead to settle in VT-17 (UN).

Drawn faithfully, and not

The composer, its field note and the warning mark are the locked voice design’s (voice-composer.html); the notice line is the port’s (ComposerView.cs: subheadline, the warning colour, one line, cut at the end). Giving the notice line a link is new (ED). The composer light and the voice glow are left out; the orb is a still.

Q-V-6 B

The countdown in the pill: which look?

Already pickedThe pill counts down the last 30 s before the 5-minute cap, then finishes the text as if you had clicked the orb (OD-0099, V-7.12). The countdown starts at 4:30 of listening.

The pill is the locked glass pill in the composer (OD-0091, V-12.2 to V-12.4): the cross at its far end, the words with their sweep, the orb at its right. Play runs the last 33 seconds; the frames below are the same look at 0:30, 0:10, 0:00 and finishing.

Look
Speed
ATime left, in place of the words
From 0:30 the label reads “0:30 left” and counts each second; at the cap it turns to Finishing…. Listening and Transcribing are not shown in those 30 s.
BTime after the wordsRecommended
The label keeps Listening… or Transcribing… and adds the time after an en space in the lighter weight, the way the pill already names the language (V-12.10, no dot). The pill widens once, by the time’s width.Why: It reuses the pattern the pill already has for the language name, so it adds no new kind of thing, and you still see whether Marrow is listening or writing.

The words stay as they are. A 2 px line inside the pill’s foot, from the cross to the orb, shortens over the 30 s and takes the warning colour for the last 10. No digits.
D“Stops in”, amber at the end
The label says what will happen: “Stops in 0:30”. The digits turn the warning colour for the last 10 s. Listening and Transcribing are not shown in those 30 s.

What is fixed in all four

The pill’s width is the label’s measured width plus 88 px (V-12.2) and eases over 0.42 s when the words change; the figures are tabular, so the pill keeps still while they count. At 0:00 the label crossfades to Finishing… as at a second click (V-15). Under Reduce Motion the figures change without moving and the line steps once a second.

With the language in the pill

When the language is named in the pill (language default B, V-12.10), B reads “Transcribing… English 0:30”: the name, then the time, each after an en space in the lighter weight. A and D replace the words, so the name goes for those 30 s; C keeps it.

Not on this page

Whether a screen reader hears the countdown (for example once at 0:30 and once at 0:10) is not designed; the lead proposes one polite announcement at each of those two moments (ED). The cross-beside-the-orb style has no pill, so it has no countdown in any look.

Q-V-9 C

The CUDA offer in the hardware card: which look?

Already pickedThe installer ships the Vulkan and processor runtimes only. On NVIDIA PCs the hardware check offers the CUDA runtime as a separate download (OD-0099). It is fetched only when you ask: the Whisper.net CUDA 12 pack from nuget.org and NVIDIA’s own libraries from NVIDIA’s servers, never hosted by Marrow, checked by hash, and a half-installed pack is never loaded (OD-0104).

The card is Settings > Voice typing > Speech model as drawn (V-16.3.2) on your desktop’s probe. Choose a state, or press Play to download it. Not NVIDIA shows a PC where nothing is offered.

Progress, cancel and the end states use the model list’s own state column and 2 px bar (V-16.3.3).Why: It is the page’s own row (title, detail, capsule) and the model list’s progress column, so it needs no new control and has room for the size and both sources (OD-0104).

A short paragraph at the foot of the card, in the note’s own small type, ending with a Download CUDA link.

The size: 644 MB

The sum of the three pinned files in voice-pins.json: the Whisper.net CUDA 12 pack 1.9.1 (250,058,371 bytes, nuget.org), NVIDIA’s cuBLAS 12.4.2.65 (391,101,865 bytes) and CUDA runtime 12.4.99 (2,474,121 bytes), shown the way the model list shows sizes (V-9.2). The space it takes on disk is not measured yet (the pack’s own five files are 566 MB).

To confirm: when it starts working

Whisper.net loads its native runtime once per process (VT-6 item 5), and the hardware check itself loads one. So after the download CUDA may only be used from Marrow’s next start; the Installed state here says “Restart nth to use it” on that reading (UN, checked on the real PC, BUILD-PLAN section 4 item D). After that the GPU backend row reads “CUDA, working” (V-10.1).

No speed claim

Vulkan has not been timed against CUDA on your RTX 4080 yet (section 4 item D decides how strongly the offer is worded), so no look promises it is faster. The reason line and the model are the design’s examples (V-10.2, V-10.4). Shown only on an NVIDIA graphics card with a working probe (VT-17).
