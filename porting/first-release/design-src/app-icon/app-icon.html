Marrow’s app icon: twenty directions

An icon tells you what the app is like inside. These are twenty original directions, each a different idea, material and colour story, drawn in code at the real Windows sizes. Every card shows the icon large with its launch animation (the basis for the loading animation, made after you pick) and its idle loop, then its 16 to 256 px renders, the 16 and 24 px pixels exactly as Windows draws them, and the icon in the taskbar, Start, Alt-Tab and on the desktop. How the sizes are drawn

Each direction has its own simplified art for the small sizes (16 and 20 px, then 24 and 32 px), as a real icon set would: fewer shapes, no hairlines, edges on whole pixels. The rows marked true pixels and the 8x loupes are drawn one canvas pixel per Windows pixel, as at 100% display scale. The shipped .ico and the app package images would be exported from the picked direction at each size.

The animations

Every launch lasts 0.8 to 1.2 s and ends exactly on the still icon, so nothing jumps when it stops. Every idle loop is seamless and starts and ends on the still icon. Under Reduce Motion (Windows: Settings, Accessibility, Visual effects, Animation effects off) the launch is a plain fade and there is no idle loop; the Motion switch above previews both.

What the contexts are

The taskbar, Start, Alt-Tab and the desktop are drawn approximations of Windows 11 at 100% scale, not Microsoft’s artwork. Neutral grey tiles stand in for other apps. The desktop wallpaper is the approved Dichroic static wallpaper from the wallpaper library, in its dark and light looks.

Every icon is original and drawn in code (Canvas 2D), with no images, fonts or marks of any company. Your pick stays in this browser only, if it allows storing it. Every change on this page is transitioned (OD-0076).
