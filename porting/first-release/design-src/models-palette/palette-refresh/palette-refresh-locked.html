Why here

This is where your own OD-0067 proposal put it. Appearance already holds the theme and the accent, and the Customization page says Appearance is "about how Marrow looks".

How it behaves

  • The coloured dots are a few real makers. They glide to their new shades when the refresh lands.
  • The progress line under the row is the one the Updates page uses.
  • The summary appears below the row and stays until you leave the page.

Trade-off

It doesn't happen when you press "Update everything". It is a separate button.
