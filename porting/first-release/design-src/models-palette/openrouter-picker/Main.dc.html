OpenRouter in the model list. OpenRouter's models sit in the composer's own model list, under the maker that made each one. The search field filters as you type, across model names, ids and makers; several words narrow it further.

Colours. Makers the app already knows keep their colours. A maker it doesn't know gets one worked out from its logo, shifted to a free shade when it would collide (Mistral beside Cursor, Moonshot beside Grok).

Placement. The list opens upward from the model chip and stops 10 px above it, so it never covers the chip. While the list scrolls, its top and bottom edges fade softly instead of cutting rows off.

Motion. Nothing cuts. As you type, rows that stop matching fold away and the card eases down onto the chip; deleting letters unfolds them again. The count, the clear button, the check mark and the chip's name fade in and out.

Count. The number of models shown: 17 models, then 7 models for "k" and 1 model for "ki". With a search, the top match is highlighted; the arrow keys move the highlight and Return picks it.
