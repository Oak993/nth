Voice Composer

The composer with its voice orb. Hover the orb, click it and speak (here a simulated voice speaks for you): the orb opens sideways into a small pill that says Listening or Transcribing, your words are written into the composer as you speak, and the pill's cross cancels. Click the orb again to finish; the text stays for you to send. Siri Wave ships by default; Voice Membrane is the other choice in Settings > Customization. While you speak the composer glows from its bottom edge with your voice.

The orb at its sizes CSS px; the source reads down to about 40

The orbs are the source's own, unchanged; only their size is set, through the source's own radius, and the limb's softness through its own Edge Softness, so the edge stays about one and a half device pixels at any size. They are drawn at twice their pixels below 128 and filtered down.

How listening shows

B, pill in the composer (the default): the orb stays where it is and opens to its left into a capsule of Marrow glass: frosted over a blurred, saturated view of the composer and its voice glow, a hairline rim lit from the top with the knob's faint pink and blue fringe, a brighter lens at each rounded end, and the source's 2.35 s shine across the label (light theme: a bright frosted pill with a dark label). The label reads Listening… in silence and Transcribing… while you speak; it goes back to Listening only after 0.6 s of quiet, so pauses between words never flicker it. Finishing… covers the final pass. The cross sits inside the pill at its far end: it belongs to the voice control, stays clear of the send button, and is still to the orb's left as the amendment asks. The text field gives the pill room, its end eased, and the line scrolls to keep the newest word in view.

C, floating pill: the orb lifts out of its slot into a capsule 8 px above the composer, which opens to the right over the toolbar so it stays clear of the chat content above (the files strip is centred). The text field keeps its full width. A, cross: no pill; a cross slides out from behind the orb.

Closing (the second click, the cross, Esc) folds the pill back into the orb: the label fades first, then the capsule narrows onto the orb and fades as it arrives.

Where the attachment goes

Inside (default): at the pill's leading edge, the same chip button (34 by 28, capsule, hover fill as the Mac's .chip), inset 8 px like the send button on the other end, centred on the controls' line. The text starts 6 px after it. It sits on the bottom line with the other controls, so it stays put when the text grows upward (ComposerView :143).

Outside: a round glass button in the 60 px gutter, centred on the same line, so the text keeps its 14 px inset. Where the pill fills the column, the pill gives the button 44 px.

How the voice drives the orb and the words

The level (0 to 1) goes through the source's own smoothing (attack 0.07 s, release 0.24 s, orb-audio.ts), then the source's audio rules: warp +0.85 x mid, contour +0.075 x low, rim highlight +0.16 x high, exposure up to x1.12, times the preset's strength (Siri 0.8, Membrane 1.0).

Speed is the owner's rule: at the resting speed in a pause, rising with the voice through a second follower (attack 0.12 s, release 0.45 s, so it never jitters) up to 3.2 times rest, and 4.6 times rest while transcribing, the fastest of all. The phase is integrated, so no change of speed ever jumps the wave.

Words arrive 0.3 to 0.7 s after they are spoken, in order. The newest is lighter until the next one confirms it; a word heard wrong at first ("team", "tool") is revised in place, its width easing. The second click starts a short final pass: words spoken but not yet written come in, the full stop settles.

Voice glow on the composer

voice-glow 0.2.1 by Jakub Antalik (MIT), ported as the beam was: the package's own stylesheet generator and frame driver run unchanged (the stylesheet is byte for byte the package's), only its React wrapper is replaced by a plain mount. It is fitted to the 820 by 52 pill with the package's own props: scale 0.7, and the lobes, their spacing and the range widened 2.4 times, so the glow spans the composer and the transcribing sweep runs into both bottom corners.

Open questions for the owner

  • Locked as the default (OD-0091): B, the glass pill in the composer, with Siri Wave at 32 px and the attachment inside. The other choices stay as user settings in Customization: listening style Pill, Cross or Floating; orb Siri Wave or Voice Membrane; size 32 or 26; attachment inside or outside.
  • The pill's words: Listening…, Transcribing…, Finishing…. Alternatives: Hearing you… for Listening, Writing… for Transcribing.
  • Live words need streaming recognition. Whisper transcribes in windows of audio, so the app re-transcribes a sliding window and revises the newest words, as shown; the engine and its delay are to be researched before building.
  • While you speak, should the Border beam give way (shown) or stay on under the voice glow? Switch it in the Customization card.
  • The voice glow's fit: wide (as shown) or the package's own narrow centre.

Orbs: Siri Wave and Voice Membrane are Liquid Orb Editor's own Copy-code export (LerSent001, MIT), run in WebGL2 instead of WebGPU; side by side with the export they match to within 1/255 per channel. Composer light: border-beam 1.4.1 by Jakub Antalik (MIT), the Composer Lights playground's port. Voice glow: voice-glow 0.2.1 by Jakub Antalik (MIT). No microphone is used on this page; the level comes from a built-in speech envelope or from an audio file you load, decoded on your device.
