
## ED-182

[UI] How a Lucide icon stands in for an SF Symbol's size, weight and rendering (F.SymbolImage stop, questions Q1 to Q6; OD-0010, PA-0012, ED-179). The pinned source gives only the SF request (a font, a weight, an image scale, a .fill name, a rendering mode, an effect); Apple's symbol metrics are not in it, and Lucide has one outline style. DECISION, every value parked as oracle O-63 (to be measured against the eight tour screenshots of OD-0004 in the verification wave): (Q1) A symbol drawn with a font of point size S occupies a square of side 1.2 S (Lucide's 24-unit box, whose drawing spans about 20 units, so the drawn glyph is about S tall, the height of an SF glyph at that size), vertically centred on the text's cap-height centre when it sits in a line of text, otherwise centred in its frame. Without a font the SwiftUI default body size applies. (Q2) Weight does not change the stroke: Lucide's 2 of 24 units at every weight (a named visual compromise; SF's per-weight strokes are not Lucide's design). (Q3) .imageScale multiplies the Q1 square by 0.8 (.small), 1.0 (.medium) and 1.25 (.large). (Q4) A `.fill` name draws its container (the outermost closed shape whose bounds enclose every other element) filled with the foreground colour, and every other element knocked out of that fill (clear), as SF's monochrome fill variants draw; a name whose Lucide icon has no enclosing closed shape draws its outline. (Q5) composed_rendering: the inner glyph is scaled to 50 % of the box and centred in the container; a slash is Lucide's own "-off" stroke from (2,2) to (22,22); a corner badge is a filled circle of radius 4 centred at (18,6) with the underlying drawing knocked out 1.5 units around it; text in a circle or square is drawn in Inter Semibold at 11 of 24 units, centred. (Q6) .symbolRenderingMode draws monochrome in the foreground colour (named compromise: no hierarchical or multicolour layers); .symbolEffect and .contentTransition(.symbolEffect) on these images are deferred as tracked incomplete work (`// MARROW_DEFERRED ED-182` at the call sites; the image draws statically until the verification wave). ENGINEERING DECISION; named visual compromises in (Q2) and (Q6).
